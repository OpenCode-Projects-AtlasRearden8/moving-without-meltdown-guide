# The first-night box

One box (or two) that travels in your own car, never on the truck, and
holds everything a human needs at 9 p.m. in a house made of stacked
cardboard. Pack it in week 2, tape it shut, and write

> **"FIRST NIGHT — DO NOT TRUCK"** on every side, including ones
> you'll never check, because movers are thorough people.

## The night itself

- [ ] Sheets and a blanket or sleeping bag for every bed
- [ ] Pillows (they ride fine in trash bags labeled per person)
- [ ] Phone chargers — one per person, plus a power strip
- [ ] Toiletries: toothbrushes, soap, toilet paper (**a roll in every bathroom, visible**), towels
- [ ] Medications and glasses/contact supplies
- [ ] Change of clothes for tomorrow for everyone
- [ ] Two knives, a fork and spoon per person, one small cutting board
- [ ] Plates and cups (paper is fine; dignity is week +1's problem)
- [ ] Coffee setup: the machine, the mugs, the beans. Morning-you will send a thank-you note
- [ ] Snacks, water bottles, and something easy for dinner the first night
- [ ] Basic toolkit: screwdriver, box cutter, scissors, tape, command hooks, a light bulb or two
- [ ] Flashlight or headlamp (fuse boxes do not wait politely)
- [ ] Cleaning kit: trash bags, all-purpose spray, paper towels, dish soap, a sponge
- [ ] Documents wallet: IDs, lease or closing papers, insurance cards — or your whole [moving folder](01-eight-week-countdown.html)

## Day-two convenience (a second box is allowed)

- [ ] Shower curtain and rings, or the towel you will hang in the doorway like a curtain and briefly love
- [ ] Wi-Fi password card from the previous residents (ask for it, it's a kindness)
- [ ] A single extension cord
- [ ] Kids' one beloved toy per kid (and the pet's bowls, leash, food — pets notice unpacking schedules)
- [ ] Good scissors. Not moving scissors. *Good* scissors.

## The rule that makes the box work

If it's on this list, it never touches a moving truck, stays with you,
and gets unpacked at 8:32 p.m. before anything else, including the
beds. Everything else on [the countdown](01-eight-week-countdown.html)
can wait for its week. This box cannot.
