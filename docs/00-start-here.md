# Start here (take a breath first)

Hi — you're moving, and you found a manual that promises *no meltdowns*.
Bold claim for a move. Here's the whole idea in one breath:

> Moving is a pile of small, boring tasks pretending to be one giant
> scary task. This guide splits the pile into small pieces, puts each
> piece on a specific week, and hands you the exact words to say when
> you call real humans.

Nothing here touches the internet. No accounts, no tracking, no "log in
to continue." You can read this on a laptop with the Wi-Fi off, and
probably should at least once, because it's very peaceful.

## The three rules of moving without meltdowns

1. **Boring beats brilliant.** A to-do list done at 8 p.m. on Tuesday
   beats a perfect plan you'd get around to this weekend. Aspiration is
   the enemy of a deposit.
2. **Photos on everything.** Serial numbers, meters, walls, the day the
   couch does something weird. Future-you uses these constantly.
3. **One box decides the whole day.** Pack the [first-night box](06-first-night-box.html)
   first and label it like it owes you money.

## Two honest words about scope

This is life-admin, not legal or financial advice, and it's written for
the common case in the United States (sorry, DMV-by-every-other-name).
Where rules vary by state or your lease, the guide tells you to check
the actual paper — because the actual paper wins.
