# Cost worksheet

Every move-shaped cost on one page, so "what did this all cost" is an
answered question and not a hope. Print this page, fill in by hand,
zoom out once a week. Numbers you don't know yet get a guess marked
"?" — guessing is allowed, blank rows are fine, being surprised by
$900 is not.

## The weekly ritual

Once a week during the countdown, spend five minutes: fill "Paid" for
anything you paid this week, update guesses, look at the total once,
and then close the tab. A worksheet checked weekly stops being scary;
one you avoid for six weeks is how meltdowns are manufactured.

Two nudges built into the math: decluttering lowers the mover line
(and the storage one), and the [utility overlap trick](03-utility-transfer-scripts.html)
costs about two nights of bills — cheap, on purpose.

*This worksheet is budgeting help, not financial advice; your lease,
movers, and closing papers set the real numbers.*
