# The eight-week countdown

Eight weeks sounds long. It is not long. It is *exactly enough time if
you start at week eight*, which is why this page exists. Do the tiny
version of each item the week it appears; details live on their own
pages.

> Print this page or the [single-page checklist](checklist.html) and
> stick it on the fridge. Fridge placements correlate strongly with
> things actually happening. (Citation: every household ever.)

## Week 8 — the quiet get-ready week

- [ ] Start a moving folder (paper or digital). Everything goes in it.
- [ ] Decide your budget with the [cost worksheet](09-cost-worksheet.html).
- [ ] Decide: movers, truck-and-friends, or hybrid. Read [quotes, decoded](04-mover-quotes-decoded.html).
- [ ] Get two or three written mover quotes. No verbal-only quotes. Ever.
- [ ] Create your [address-change master list](02-address-change-master-list.html) — just the list, don't call yet.
- [ ] Start a donation box. Things leaving early make week 4 easier.

## Week 7 — the paperwork week

- [ ] Request time off work (even if you're "pretty sure" you'll need it).
- [ ] Book movers or reserve the truck. **Do not wait.** Prices only move in one direction.
- [ ] Confirm what your [lease or closing papers](05-rental-walk-through-photos.html) say about notice, deposits, and walk-throughs.
- [ ] Send written notice to your landlord if required (check lease terms).
- [ ] Collect important documents into one folder: IDs, lease, closing docs, vet records, insurance.

## Week 6 — the admin sweep

- [ ] Call insurers (renters, home, auto) with the [master list](02-address-change-master-list.html).
- [ ] Call banks and credit card companies.
- [ ] Schedule utility stop/start dates using the [transfer scripts](03-utility-transfer-scripts.html).
- [ ] Ask your pharmacy about transferring prescriptions to a new location; get refills you'll need across move week.
- [ ] Book the elevator, parking spot, or loading dock if your buildings require one.

## Week 5 — the getting-boxed week

- [ ] Gather boxes, tape, paper, markers. Buy fewer than you think, then buy one more pack later. (You will. Everyone does.)
- [ ] Begin [packing by zone](07-packing-by-zone.html), starting with storage, guest rooms, and the zone that never gets opened.
- [ ] Sort out-of-season clothes. Donate the "maybe someday" pile today.
- [ ] Start consuming pantry and freezer food like it's a sport.

## Week 4 — the addresses and the arms

- [ ] File your USPS official change-of-address and [sweep the master list](02-address-change-master-list.html) (IRS, voters, subscriptions).
- [ ] Update the DMV / driver's license and vehicle registration per your state.
- [ ] Update voter registration if moving states or precincts.
- [ ] Confirm movers in writing: date, times, quote number, what's included.
- [ ] Order: printer labels or index cards, mattress bags, and bandages for edges you'll carry around corners. (You know the corner.)

## Week 3 — the deep middle

- [ ] Keep packing, one zone per evening. It's a marathon made of sprints.
- [ ] Confirm utility start dates **at the new place** in writing.
- [ ] Arrange child/pet care for moving day if applicable.
- [ ] Take the [walk-through photo set](05-rental-walk-through-photos.html) of your **current** place, good rooms included.
- [ ] Back up computers and phones. Fully. Not "mostly."

## Week 2 — the almost-done week

- [ ] Pack everything except: everyday kitchen, bathroom, bed, and your [first-night box](06-first-night-box.html).
- [ ] Drain and prep appliances per their manuals (dishwashers unhook and drain, freezers defrost — a freezer left frozen will remember, loudly)
- [ ] Assemble the [first-night box](06-first-night-box.html) and the [packing-by-zone](07-packing-by-zone.html) "do not pack" shelf.
- [ ] Reconfirm movers, truck, and helpers. A confirmation text today saves a wobble later.
- [ ] Pay any final special pickups: donations, e-waste, that exercise bike.

## Week 1 — the fine-tune week

- [ ] Confirm a move-day schedule by the hour: load, drive, unload.
- [ ] Photograph meters on the out and in (see [scripts](03-utility-transfer-scripts.html)).
- [ ] Pack the last-use kit: dishes for duty, towels, toiletries, chargers.
- [ ] Do a final [walk-through photo pass](05-rental-walk-through-photos.html) at the old place after it's empty.
- [ ] Cash or card handy for [tips](04-mover-quotes-decoded.html), plus water and snacks for the crew — kindness is on page [04].

## Moving day — the day it all goes

- [ ] [First-night box](06-first-night-box.html) rides with **you**, not the truck.
- [ ] Direct at the door / confirm inventory against the mover's list.
- [ ] Photograph walls, floors, and meters at the new place before the crew is done.
- [ ] Test water, power, stove, internet; note anything broken immediately, in writing.
- [ ] Sign the paperwork only after you've seen the numbers you agreed to.

## Week +1 — the gentle landing

Nothing is due. Read [the gentle unpack order](08-gentle-unpack-order.html),
unpack one zone per evening, and let the rest wait its turn.
