# Address-change master list

The move isn't done when the boxes are done. It's done when *the mail,
the money, and the state* know where you live. This is the sweep list.
Work it in batches by category — five minutes a batch, done by week 4.

> The trick that makes this page work: **do one category per sitting.**
> Ten banks is a project. "Bank" is a Tuesday-night task.

## Money: banks, cards, and investing

- [ ] Checking and savings banks (app address update + one call if unsure)
- [ ] Credit card companies (all of them, even the quilt one)
- [ ] Loan servicers: student loans, mortgage, car (paper still arrives)
- [ ] Investment and retirement accounts (they hold the annual letters)
- [ ] Insurance: renters/home, auto, life, health — **update all addresses AND check coverage for the new address**. (Why: a new address can change rates, and car insurance for a new state is its own chapter.)
- [ ] Payroll / HR at work (pay stubs and year-end forms)
- [ ] FSA, HSA, or benefits platforms

## Government: the state will find you

- [ ] USPS official change-of-address (usps.com or the post office; takes 2 minutes, forwards most mail, catches stragglers)
- [ ] IRS and state tax: use Form 8822 (federal) and your state's equivalent so refunds and letters land at the right doorstep
- [ ] State tax authority / state DMV for driver's license and vehicle registration — rules and deadline windows vary **a lot** by state; check your new state's page
- [ ] Voter registration: if you moved precincts or states, re-register. Voting is the one address update with a deadline you can't reschedule.
- [ ] Social Security Administration (if you receive benefits)
- [ ] Library cards: new library, old library.

## Daily life: the places that actually matter day one

- [ ] Pharmacy: transfer prescriptions to a pharmacy **near the new address**; ask early — some transfers are one-way
- [ ] Primary care doctor, dentist, other care providers: address update + records transfer forms
- [ ] Vet + microchip registration (pets move too)
- [ ] Schools, childcare, or caregiver directories: also confirm your health plan has them in-network at the new address
- [ ] Gym, clubs, associations
- [ ] Cellular carrier (cheap, easy, do it)
- [ ] Internet provider: **new order + old cancel** — see the [utility scripts](03-utility-transfer-scripts.html) for the overlap trick
- [ ] Cable/streaming isn't urgent, but billing address is

## Subscriptions and stragglers

- [ ] Amazon and other shopping addresses (they love defaulting to old ones)
- [ ] Magazine and box subscriptions
- [ ] Card hygiene: update any bill that autopays from a card that closed or got reissued during the move
- [ ] Landlord back home for the deposit forward (paper lives forever)
- [ ] Friends and family who still send cards

## The two-minute version of this list

If you do nothing else from this page:

1. **USPS change-of-address** (catches most paper),
2. **Bank and card companies** (money must know),
3. **Insurers** (coverage must know),
4. **Pharmacy** (meds must know),
5. **DMV + voters** (the law and the ballot must know).

Everything else is a bonus sweep, and bonus sweeps can wait a week.
