# Packing by zone

"Packing" failed as a plan because it was never a plan — it was a
feeling about a garage. Zones fix that. A zone is any area you can
finish in an evening, then tape shut and *never open again until it's
on the floor of the right room.*

## The three laws of boxes

1. **One zone per box, one room per label.** The box's destination is
   the room, not the item. "Kitchen" tells a mover where to walk — the
   label should say what it needs to say out loud.
2. **Heavy smalls, light bigs.** Books in small boxes, pillows and
   lamps in big ones. A box you can't lift is wall art now.
3. **The plate rule: everything gets padding.** Dishes, glassware,
   electronics, mirrors, that one lamp that's mostly lamp. Paper is
   cheap; reproach is forever.

## Zone order (soonest-taped first)

Start with the zones that have the least 30-day relevance and work
toward daily life. Each zone is one evening, roughly, with [the gentle
unpack rhythm](08-gentle-unpack-order.html) waiting for every box at
the other end.

| Week | Zone                                | Notes                                                        |
|------|-------------------------------------|--------------------------------------------------------------|
| 5    | Storage, attic, garage, guest room  | Includes "remember this box? yes" boxes                      |
| 5    | Books & shelves, off-season clothes | Donate the maybe-pile today                                  |
| 4–5  | Decor, art, hobby corners           | Take down wall art early; it feels forward, it is forward    |
| 4    | Dining room & good dishes           | Wrap plates on edge, vertically, like records                |
| 3    | Coat closet, linen closet           | Vacuum bags for comforters if you have them                  |
| 2–3  | Kids' rooms & pets' things          | Keep one day's worth of toys/clothes out (see [first-night box](06-first-night-box.html)) |
| 2    | Home office, files, electronics     | Cables in one bag per device; snap a photo of the back of the TV |
| 1    | Kitchen gadgets & pantry            | Use the food. Eat the noodles.                               |
| Day 0| Bathroom, beds, everyday kitchen    | These go last, come off first                                |

## Labels that earn their ink

Write on the box: **room — contents in five words — priority number.**
"KITCHEN — mixer, mugs, cutting boards — 2." Tape a matching colored
sticker on the box and on the door frame of the destination room at
the new place. Crews follow color faster than handwriting.

Also number your boxes ("K-07") and keep one index page in your
[moving folder](01-eight-week-countdown.html) with the count per room.
An "all 23 kitchen boxes present" check beats having to guess at 6 p.m.

## The "do not pack" shelf

One shelf (or one dresser corner) where essentials stay out of every
box to the very end: keys, chargers, daily meds, kids' and pets'
daily things, cleaning sprays, scissors, the tape itself. If you pack
the tape, you have met the enemy of moving, and it is you.

## The electronics mini-checklist

- [ ] Photo of the back of the TV / desk / router *before* unplugging anything
- [ ] Cables labeled with masking tape ("TV power"), bagged per device
- [ ] Remotes, batteries, hub boxes bagged together per room
- [ ] Computers packed flat, in their cases if you have them, with you in the car

## The one honest sentence

Every zone you tape this week makes the truck lighter, the hour count
lower (see [quotes, decoded](04-mover-quotes-decoded.html)), and [the
gentle unpack](08-gentle-unpack-order.html) feel more like a pamphlet
and less like a heap.
